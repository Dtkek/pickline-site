Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

Pickline ("we") is a personal, non-commercial project that publishes Dota 2 content to a Telegram channel and to the TikTok account of its owner.

What the Pickline TikTok integration does

The integration is used only by the owner of the Pickline TikTok account. It uploads videos that we create ourselves to that account as drafts, so the owner can review and publish them in the TikTok app.

Data we access

  • Basic profile information of the connected TikTok account (open ID, display name), to confirm which account is connected.
  • Access and refresh tokens issued by TikTok, stored privately and used only to upload drafts.

We do not access followers, messages, viewing history or any other user data, and we do not process data of any other TikTok users.

Sharing

We do not sell or share any data with third parties. Tokens are never published.

Removal

The owner can revoke access at any time in TikTok settings (Security → Manage app permissions). Questions: via t.me/pickline_dota2.
